Delete your Edict account

Edict is the voice launcher for Android by Joshua Brisebois. Your Edict messages account is the account you signed in to with your phone number.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Edict and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Messages account.
  3. Tap Delete my messages account.
  4. Type delete to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Your phone is signed out right away, and message notifications stop on all your devices.

Delete it by email

If you no longer have the app, email SUPPORT-EMAIL-TO-BE-SET with the subject “Delete my Edict account” and the phone number you signed in with. We will check that the number is yours before anything is deleted, then start the same deletion for you.

The 14 days

Deleting signs you out everywhere at once. Your account is then held for 14 days in case you change your mind.

  • Sign in again with a code within 14 days and the deletion is cancelled. Your account stays as it was.
  • After 14 days everything is erased for good and cannot be brought back.

What is deleted

This deletes your whole account from the server, not only from your phone. It is the same account the Grounders apps use, so it is deleted there too.

  • Your name and phone number
  • The messages, voice messages, photos and files you sent
  • Your contacts and contact requests
  • Conversations and groups you started, for everyone in them
  • Your place in groups other people started
  • Your call history
  • Your Grounders posts, with their photos, video and audio
  • The connections you gave to AI assistants to read your messages

What is not deleted

  • A copy of something you sent that another person saved to their own notes stays with them.
  • A call recording you chose to keep in Offhand stays in your Offhand account. Delete it there.
  • Edict's settings and anything stored only on your phone stay until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
